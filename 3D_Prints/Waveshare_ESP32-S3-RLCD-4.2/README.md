# Enclosure for Waveshare ESP32-S3-RLCD-4.2

[Waveshare
ESP32-S3-RLCD-4.2](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-RLCD-4.2)
has a very fragile screen, so an enclosure is required.

This enclosure consists of two 3mm 3d-prointed panels in front and on
the back of the device, spaced by 4 stand-offs.

Part list:

* M2.5*8mm screws: 4 pc (back panel to the device)
* M2.5*6mm screws: 4 pc (front panel to stand-offs)
* M2.5*15mm Male-Female stand-offs: 4pc
* M2.5 nuts: 4pc (back panel to stand-offs)

Alternatively, Female-Femnale stand-offs can be used, and screws for attaching the back panel.

Panels print: PLA, 0.2mm, 20% infill.
