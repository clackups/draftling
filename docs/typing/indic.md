# Typing guide: Indic pack (Devanagari, Bengali, Telugu and Tamil)

Layouts: US plus four Devanagari, three Bengali, five Telugu and four
Tamil ones.
US, HI-IN and TA99 are active from the start; turn the others on in F1 -> Settings ->
Keyboard -> Active layouts. **Ctrl+L** or **Win+Space** switches
between the active layouts.

| Tag | Layout | Best for |
|-----|--------|----------|
| HI-IN | Hindi InScript (Government of India standard) | Hindi, Sanskrit and the other Devanagari languages |
| MR-IN | Marathi InScript | Marathi (adds eyelash ra and candra a) |
| NE-TR | Nepali Traditional | Nepali typists used to the traditional layout |
| NE-RO | Nepali Romanized | letters on the keys of their Latin sound |
| BN-IN | Bengali InScript (Government of India standard) | Bengali and Assamese, users of the Indian InScript layouts |
| BN-PR | Bengali Probhat | letters on the keys of their Latin sound |
| BN-NJ | Bengali National (Jatiya, the Bangladesh standard) | typists used to the Bijoy key positions |
| TE-IN | Telugu InScript (Government of India standard) | users of the Indian InScript layouts |
| TE-SA | Telugu Sarala | fast Telugu typing, the frequent letters on the home row |
| TE-RT | Telugu RTS (phonetic) | typing Telugu words in Latin letters |
| TE-PO | Telugu Pothana | one key per letter, mostly by sound |
| TE-AP | Telugu Apple | typists used to Apple's Telugu keyboard |
| TA99 | Tamil 99 (Government of Tamil Nadu standard) | most Tamil typists |
| TA-TW | Tamil Typewriter | trained typewriter typists |
| TA-IN | Tamil InScript (Government of India standard) | users of the Indian InScript layouts |
| TA-TV | Taravinyas | typing Tamil by place and manner of articulation |

## Devanagari

All four are plain layouts: type in the order the letters are spoken
-- consonant, then its vowel sign -- and the editor draws the syllable
(the i sign before its consonant, conjuncts, the reph above). To join
two consonants, type the virama (halant) between them: `k` `d` `m` on
InScript gives k-virama-sa, drawn as one conjunct.

### HI-IN and MR-IN: InScript

Vowel signs on the left hand (with Shift: the vowels), consonants on
the right; the number row types the Devanagari digits.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `e f r g t s w a q` | aa, i, ii, u, uu, e, ai, o, au signs |
| Shift + `D E F R G T S W A Q` | a aa i ii u uu e ai o au |
| `d` / `x` / Shift+`X` / Shift+`-` | virama / anusvara / candrabindu / visarga |
| `k` Shift+`K` `i` Shift+`I` Shift+`U` | ka kha ga gha nga |
| `;` Shift+`;` `p` Shift+`P` Shift+`]` | ca cha ja jha nya |
| `'` Shift+`'` `[` Shift+`[` Shift+`C` | Ta Tha Da Dha Na |
| `l` Shift+`L` `o` Shift+`O` `v` | ta tha da dha na |
| `h` Shift+`H` `y` Shift+`Y` `c` | pa pha ba bha ma |
| `/` `j` `n` `b` `M` `<` `m` `u` | ya ra la va sha ssa sa ha |
| `]` / `=` / Shift+`.` | nukta / vocalic r sign / danda |
| Shift + `3 4 5 6 7 8` | ra below (rakar), reph, jnya, tra, ksha, shra |
| AltGr + `1` / `2` | zero-width joiner / non-joiner |

Examples: `u f x o r` = hindi, `v c m d l s` = namaste, `Y e j l` =
bharat. Shift+`N` types lla. MR-IN differs only in a few Marathi
letters: Shift+`J` eyelash ra and Shift+`1` candra a.

### NE-TR: Nepali Traditional

The layout of Nepali typewriters and the Preeti font (as m17n
"ne-trad"). Example: `g ] k f n` = nepal.

### NE-RO: Nepali Romanized

Letters on the keys of their Latin sound: `k` ka, `g` ga, `n` na,
`a` aa sign, `i` i sign, `e` e sign, `o` o sign, `/` virama, Shift for
the aspirated or retroflex letter (`K` kha, `T` tha, `N` Na). Examples:
`n e p a l` = nepal, `n m s / t e` = namaste.

## Bengali

All three are typed in the order the letters are spoken -- consonant,
then its vowel sign, also for the i, e, ai, o and au signs that are
drawn before or around the consonant -- and the editor draws the
syllable. Join two consonants with the hasanta (virama) between them:
`k` `d` Shift+`<` on InScript gives k-hasanta-ssa, drawn as the
conjunct ksha.

### BN-IN: InScript

The same key positions as Hindi InScript: vowel signs on the left hand
(with Shift: the vowels), consonants on the right; the number row
types the Bengali digits.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `e f r g t = s w a q` | aa, i, ii, u, uu, ri, e, ai, o, au signs |
| Shift + `D E F R G T + S W A Q` | a aa i ii u uu ri e ai o au |
| `d` / `x` / Shift+`X` / Shift+`-` | hasanta / anusvara / candrabindu / visarga |
| `k` Shift+`K` `i` Shift+`I` Shift+`U` | ka kha ga gha nga |
| `;` Shift+`;` `p` Shift+`P` Shift+`]` | ca cha ja jha nya |
| `'` Shift+`'` `[` Shift+`[` Shift+`C` | Ta Tha Da Dha Na |
| `l` Shift+`L` `o` Shift+`O` `v` | ta tha da dha na |
| `h` Shift+`H` `y` Shift+`Y` `c` | pa pha ba bha ma |
| Shift+`?` `j` `n` `M` `<` `m` `u` | ya ra la sha ssa sa ha |
| `/` / Shift+`J` / `z` / `]` | yya / rha / khanda ta / nukta |
| Shift + `.` / AltGr + `.` | danda / double danda |
| Shift + `1 3 4 5 6 7 8` | a-ya-aa, ra-phala, reph, jnya, tra, ksha, shra |
| AltGr + `/` | ya-phala |
| AltGr + `1` / `2` | zero-width joiner / non-joiner |

Examples: `y e x n e` = bangla, `Y e j l` = bharat, Shift+`E` `c` `f`
= ami.

### BN-NJ: National (Jatiya)

The key positions of Bijoy, typed in Unicode order. A vowel key types
the vowel sign right after a consonant and the independent vowel
anywhere else; after the hasanta `g` it types the independent vowel
too, which is how a vowel letter follows a consonant.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `h d D s S a c C x X` | aa, i, ii, u, uu, ri, e, ai, o, au (sign or letter) |
| Shift+`H` | a |
| `g` / Shift+`Q` / `z` / Shift+`Z` | hasanta / anusvara / candrabindu / visarga |
| `j` Shift+`J` `o` Shift+`O` `q` | ka kha ga gha nga |
| `y` Shift+`Y` `u` Shift+`U` Shift+`I` | ca cha ja jha nya |
| `t` Shift+`T` `e` Shift+`E` Shift+`B` | Ta Tha Da Dha Na |
| `k` Shift+`K` `l` Shift+`L` `b` | ta tha da dha na |
| `r` Shift+`R` `f` Shift+`F` `m` | pa pha ba bha ma |
| `w` `v` Shift+`V` Shift+`M` Shift+`N` `n` `i` | ya ra la sha ssa sa ha |
| `p` Shift+`P` Shift+`W` AltGr+`k` | rra rha yya khanda ta |
| Shift+`G` / `g` Shift+`G` | danda / double danda |
| AltGr + `f` / AltGr + Shift+`F` | Assamese ra / wa |
| `` ` `` / Shift+`` ` `` | zero-width non-joiner / joiner |

Examples: `f h Q V h` = bangla, `h m d` = ami, `j g d` = ka i (koi),
`j g N` = ksha.

## Telugu

InScript and Sarala are plain layouts; RTS, Pothana and Apple type on
the US keyboard through an input method. All are typed in the order
the letters are spoken --
consonant, then its vowel sign -- and the editor draws the syllable,
with the vowel sign joined to the consonant and the second consonant
of a conjunct below the first (the vattu). Join two consonants with
the virama between them: `m` `d` `l` on InScript gives s-virama-ta,
drawn as sta.

### TE-IN: InScript

The same key positions as Hindi InScript: vowel signs on the left hand
(with Shift: the vowels), consonants on the right; the number row
types the Telugu digits.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `e f r g t = z s w` `` ` `` `a q` | aa, i, ii, u, uu, ri, e, ee, ai, o, oo, au signs |
| Shift + `D E F R G T + Z S W ~ A Q` | a aa i ii u uu ri e ee ai o oo au |
| `d` / `x` / Shift+`X` / Shift+`-` | virama / anusvara / candrabindu / visarga |
| `k` Shift+`K` `i` Shift+`I` Shift+`U` | ka kha ga gha nga |
| `;` Shift+`;` `p` Shift+`P` `]` | ca cha ja jha nya |
| `'` Shift+`'` `[` Shift+`[` Shift+`C` | Ta Tha Da Dha Na |
| `l` Shift+`L` `o` Shift+`O` `v` | ta tha da dha na |
| `h` Shift+`H` `y` Shift+`Y` `c` | pa pha ba bha ma |
| `/` `j` `n` `b` Shift+`M` `<` `m` `u` | ya ra la va sha ssa sa ha |
| Shift+`J` / Shift+`N` | rra / lla |
| Shift + `.` / AltGr + `.` | danda / double danda |
| Shift + `3 5 6 7 8` | ra-vattu, jnya, tra, ksha, shra |
| AltGr + `;` / AltGr + `p` | tsa / dza |
| AltGr + `1` / `2` | zero-width joiner / non-joiner |

Examples: `l z n g i g` = telugu, `v c m d l s` = namaste, Shift+`D`
`c d c` = amma.

### TE-SA: Sarala

Krishna Dhullipalla's layout for fast Telugu typing (XKB
"Telugu (Sarala)"): the frequent letters on the home row, each vowel
sign with its vowel on Shift. The number row keeps the Latin digits;
AltGr + digit types the Telugu ones.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `j i y u x` `` ` `` `q e ] z o \` | aa, i, ii, u, uu, ri, e, ee, ai, o, oo, au signs |
| Shift + `F J I Y U X ~ Q E } Z O \|` | a aa i ii u uu ri e ee ai o oo au |
| `f` / `s` / Shift+`M` / Shift+`S` | virama / anusvara / candrabindu / visarga |
| `k` Shift+`K` `g` Shift+`G` Shift+`.` | ka kha ga gha nga |
| `h` Shift+`H` `[` Shift+`[` Shift+`,` | ca cha ja jha nya |
| `;` Shift+`;` `c` Shift+`C` Shift+`N` | Ta Tha Da Dha Na |
| `t` Shift+`T` `d` Shift+`D` `n` | ta tha da dha na |
| `p` Shift+`P` `b` Shift+`B` `m` | pa pha ba bha ma |
| `a` `r` `l` `v` `/` Shift+`W` `w` Shift+`V` | ya ra la va sha ssa sa ha |
| Shift+`R` / Shift+`L` / Shift+`A` | rra / lla / avagraha |
| AltGr + `\` / AltGr + Shift+`\` | danda / double danda |
| AltGr + `-` / `=` | zero-width non-joiner / joiner |
| AltGr (+ Shift) + `[ ] ; , . /` and `` ` `` | the US punctuation of the key: `[ { ] } ; : < > / ` `` ` `` ``~`` |

Examples: `t q l u g u` = telugu, `n m w f t e` = namaste, Shift+`F`
`m f m` = amma.

### TE-RT: RTS (phonetic)

Type each word in Latin letters, the Rice Transliteration Standard
(RTS) used on Telugu websites and mailing lists; it is shown in Telugu
as you type, and the Latin letters stay in the status bar until the
word ends. Backspace removes the last Latin letter while the word is
open. A consonant shows its virama until a vowel follows.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `a aa i ee u oo R e ea ai o oe au` | a aa i ii u uu ri e ee ai o oo au (also `A I U E O`, `ii uu ae oa ou`) |
| `k kh g gh ~m` | ka kha ga gha nga |
| `c C j jh ~n` | ca cha ja jha nya |
| `T Th D Dh N` | Ta Tha Da Dha Na |
| `t th d dh n` | ta tha da dha na |
| `p ph b bh m` | pa pha ba bha ma |
| `y r l v S sh s h L ~r` | ya ra la va sha ssa sa ha lla rra |
| `x` or `ksh`, `jn` | ksha, jnya |
| `M` / `@M` / `@h` | anusvara / candrabindu / visarga |
| `n&` / `m&` | n / m that stays a letter before a consonant |
| `^` | virama + zero-width non-joiner (the explicit half form) |
| `0`-`9` / `\|` / `\|\|` | Telugu digits / danda / double danda |
| Shift + Space | zero-width non-joiner |

`n` or `m` before another consonant (except `r` and `y`) is written
as the anusvara, and so is `m` at the end of a word: `sundaram` =
sundaram with both anusvaras. Examples: `telugu`, `namaskaaram`,
`kRshNa`, `andhra`, `jnaanam`.

### TE-PO: Pothana

One key per letter, consonants with their inherent a; a vowel key
right after a consonant types the vowel sign, anywhere else the
independent vowel. `a` after a consonant ends the syllable, so the
next vowel stands alone.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `a A i I u U q Q e E Y o O [` | a aa i ii u uu ri rri e ee ai o oo au (sign or letter) |
| `h` / `f` / Shift+`F` / `]` | virama / anusvara / candrabindu / visarga |
| `k` Shift+`K` `g` Shift+`G` Shift+`M` | ka kha ga gha nga |
| `c` Shift+`C` `j` Shift+`J` Shift+`V` | ca cha ja jha nya |
| `t` Shift+`T` `z` Shift+`Z` Shift+`N` | Ta Tha Da Dha Na |
| `w` Shift+`W` `d` Shift+`D` `n` | ta tha da dha na |
| `p` Shift+`P` `b` Shift+`B` `m` | pa pha ba bha ma |
| `y` `r` `l` `v` Shift+`S` `x` `s` Shift+`H` | ya ra la va sha ssa sa ha |
| Shift+`L` / Shift+`R` / Shift+`X` | lla / rra / ksha |
| AltGr + digit / AltGr + `[ ] ; ' ...` | Telugu digits / the US punctuation of the key |

Examples: `w e l u g u` = telugu, `k h k` = kka, `k A` = kaa.

### TE-AP: Apple

The key positions of Apple's Telugu keyboard. A vowel key after a
consonant types its sign; `q` after a consonant ends the syllable.
For a conjunct the vowel may come before the halant, as on Apple's
keyboard: `j o h j` and `j h j o` both give k-virama-k-ee.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `q e r w i p W E u o [ t y ]` | a aa i ii u uu ri rri e ee ai o oo au (sign or letter) |
| `h` / `g` / Shift+`\|` / `\` | halant / anusvara / candrabindu / visarga |
| `j` Shift+`J` `x` Shift+`X` Shift+`R` | ka kha ga gha nga |
| `m` Shift+`M` `/` Shift+`/` Shift+`T` | ca cha ja jha nya |
| `z` Shift+`Z` `c` Shift+`C` Shift+`L` | Ta Tha Da Dha Na |
| `s` Shift+`S` `d` Shift+`D` `l` | ta tha da dha na |
| `;` Shift+`;` `v` Shift+`V` `b` | pa pha ba bha ma |
| `n` `k` `a` `f` Shift+`F` Shift+`'` `'` Shift+`B` | ya ra la va sha ssa sa ha |
| Shift+`A` / Shift+`K` / Shift+`Y` / Shift+`U` | lla / rra / ksha / sri |
| AltGr + digit / AltGr + `[ ] ; ' ...` | Telugu digits / the US punctuation of the key |

Example: `s u a i x i` = telugu.

## Tamil

Letters are named here by transliteration: `Ta` / `Na` = retroflex t /
n, `n2a` = the alveolar n, `zha`, `La` (retroflex l), `Ra` (alveolar r).

### TA99: Tamil 99

Vowels on the left hand, consonants on the right. A vowel after a
consonant joins it as its vowel sign.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `a q s w d e g t r c x z` | a aa i ii u uu e ee ai o oo au |
| `h b [ ] o p l ; j k` | ka nga ca nya Ta Na ta na pa ma |
| `' m n v / y u i` | ya ra la va zha La Ra n2a |
| `f` / Shift+`F` | pulli / aytham |
| Shift+`Q W E R U`, `T`, `Y` | sa ssa ja ha sha, k-ssa, sri |

Doubled consonants and nasal + stop pairs get the pulli by themselves:
`h h` = k-pulli ka, `; l` = n-pulli ta. Type `a` after a consonant to
keep the next vowel separate.

Examples: `lks/f` = tamizh, `hq` = kaa, `jhh` = pakka, `k;l` = manta.

### TA-TW: Tamil Typewriter

Type in the order the letters are seen: the e, ee and ai signs (`b`,
`n`, `i`) before the consonant, the aa piece (`h`) after it. The sign
waits on a dotted circle until its consonant comes.

| Keys | Letters |
|------|---------|
| `f ' r " l z j e g k` | ka nga ca nya Ta Na ta na pa ma |
| `a u y t H s w d` | ya ra la va zha La Ra n2a |
| `m , c v x` (Shift: aa ii uu ee oo, `I` ai) | a i u e o |
| `h p P ;` | aa sign, i sign, ii sign, pulli |
| `b n i` | e, ee, ai sign (typed first) |

Examples: `b f h` = ko, `n f h` = koo, `j k p H ;` = tamizh,
`i g` = pai.

### TA-IN: InScript

A plain layout: vowel signs on the left hand (Shift: the vowels),
consonants on the right, pulli on `d`. Type in Unicode order:
consonant first, then the vowel sign. Examples: `k e` = kaa,
`l c f` Shift+`B` `d` = tamizh. AltGr + digit types the Tamil digits.

### TA-TV: Taravinyas

`v` switches between consonant and vowel mode. The status bar shows
the mode and the next choices.

- Consonant: place (`f` velar, `d` palatal, `s` retroflex, `r`
  dental, `e` labial, `w` alveolar), then manner (`j` hard, `k`
  nasal, `l` medial, `u` lateral medial, `i` sibilant).
- Vowel: vowel (`f` a, `d` i, `s` u, `r` e, `e` o), length (`j` short,
  `k` long, `l` ai / au), then `x` for the sign or `c` for the letter.
- `a` then `j` / `k` / `l`: Grantha ja / ha / k-ssa in consonant mode;
  pulli / aytham / ZWNJ in vowel mode.
- Esc drops a half-made pick, Backspace undoes its last step.

Example, starting in consonant mode: `r j` `e k` `v` `d j x` `v`
`s l` `v` `a j` = tamizh.

## Tips

- Left / Right step over a whole syllable or conjunct; Backspace
  removes the vowel sign first, then the consonant.
- The search, file-name and WiFi fields take plain letters.
