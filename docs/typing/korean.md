# Typing guide: Korean pack

Layouts: US and KO (Korean, 2-set Hangul / Dubeolsik). Use a keyboard
with the Dubeolsik jamo printed on it, or a US keyboard and the
standard Dubeolsik positions. KO is active from the start;
**Ctrl+L** or **Win+Space** switches between US and KO.

## How it works

Each key types a jamo (consonant or vowel); they are joined into
syllable blocks as you type. A final consonant moves to the next
syllable when a vowel follows, as on any Korean keyboard.

| Keys | Result |
|------|--------|
| letter keys | jamo, composed into syllables |
| Shift + `Q W E R T` | the tense consonants (ssangbieup, ssangjieut, ssangdigeut, ssanggiyeok, ssangsiot) |
| Shift + `O P` | yae, ye |
| Backspace | removes the last jamo of the syllable being typed |

## Examples

| Keys | Word |
|------|------|
| `dkssudgktpdy` | annyeonghaseyo (hello) |
| `gksrnrdj` | hangugeo (Korean language) |
| `tkfkd` | sarang (love) |

## Tips

- Moving the cursor, a Ctrl / Alt shortcut or a layout switch finishes
  the syllable being typed.
- The search, file-name and WiFi fields take plain letters.
