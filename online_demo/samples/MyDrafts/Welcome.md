# Welcome to Draftling

This is the **online demo** of Draftling, a distraction-free writing
device. It runs the real editor firmware, compiled to WebAssembly,
on a simulated *Freenove FNK0104S* with a 4" color screen.

Your computer's keyboard stands in for the Bluetooth keyboard, and
you can click or tap the screen like the touch panel.

## Try these

- Type anything: the Markdown styling appears as you write.
- **F1** opens the menu: settings, color themes, WiFi, sync.
- **Esc** goes back to the file browser.
- **Ctrl+N** starts a new document (Markdown, Fountain or plain text).
- **Ctrl+2** splits the screen, **Ctrl+1** joins it again.
- **Ctrl+F** finds text, **Ctrl+H** finds and replaces.
- **Alt+I** shows word and line counts.
- **F10** lists every shortcut.

## Good to know

> Your documents and settings stay in this browser only.
> Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

WiFi, Git sync and USB are simulated: the menus work, but no network
or computer is involved. Some shortcuts (Ctrl+W, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+T)
belong to the browser; use the buttons under the screen for those.

Happy writing!
