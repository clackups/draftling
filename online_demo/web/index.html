Draftling in your browser

This is the real Draftling editor firmware, compiled to WebAssembly and running on a simulated device. Pick the screen and the language pack above: each combination is the firmware image that device would run. Your keyboard stands in for the Bluetooth keyboard; the mouse or your finger works the touch screen, and the buttons beside the screen are the device's own.

  • Documents are kept in this browser only and shared by every screen and language pack, like a MicroSD card moved between devices; each screen keeps its own settings. Nothing is uploaded.
  • The e-paper screen refreshes with the panel's real delays: about 0.4 s per update, and a longer full refresh after start-up. Keys typed meanwhile are queued, as on the device.
  • WiFi, Git sync and USB are simulated: the menus work, but no network or computer is involved.
  • Some shortcuts belong to the browser (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+Tab): use the buttons under the screen.
  • F10 lists every shortcut, F1 opens the menu.
  • Reset demo deletes all of the demo's documents and settings in this browser and starts a fresh demo.
