Draftling in your browser

This is the real Draftling editor firmware, compiled to WebAssembly and running on a simulated Freenove FNK0104S with its 4-inch color screen. Your keyboard stands in for the Bluetooth keyboard; the mouse or your finger works the touch screen.

  • Documents and settings are kept in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded.
  • WiFi, Git sync and USB are simulated: the menus work, but no network or computer is involved.
  • Some shortcuts belong to the browser (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+Tab): use the buttons under the screen.
  • F10 lists every shortcut, F1 opens the menu.
