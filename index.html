Draftling Web Flasher

Install Draftling firmware straight from your browser over USB — no software to install.

Web Serial isn't available in this browser. Use a desktop version of Chrome, Edge, or Opera, connected to your device over USB. (Firefox and Safari don't support Web Serial yet.)

1 Choose your board

2 Connect

Plug your device in via USB, then click Connect and pick its serial port from the browser dialog.

3 Flash

✓ Done

Firmware flashed successfully. The device is rebooting into Draftling. Files on the SD card are untouched.
